GuideRepos

Repository hub

In the panel: Repos › Repositories › a repository/repos/<forge>/<path>Needs analyses:read

One repository in depth — its health per area, how fresh every analysis is, and tabs for supply chain, quality, releases, activity, knowledge and settings. Open it from any repository link: the Repositories table, a health cell, the command palette or a chat chip. The page reads stored records only; opening it never asks the forge or a model.

The strip has one entry per health area you may read, then the head commit Holtkeeper last saw.

The header

Section titled “The header”

The header shows the avatar, path, forge, default branch and groups, then:

  • the Updates tag, saying whether changes arrive by webhook or polling. It turns into a warning when GitLab disabled the webhook. Click it to open the Updates settings.
  • Run analyzer, which queues an analyzer for this repository now.

The health strip under it has one named entry per health area you may read and ends with the head commit Holtkeeper last saw.

Change the avatar

Section titled “Change the avatar”

The avatar comes from an upload, the forge’s project or group avatar, or generated initials. With settings:write on the repository, click the large avatar to upload a PNG, JPEG, WebP or GIF of up to 512 kB, or reset it to the forge’s or the generated one. Every image of the repository reloads after a change.

Tabs

Section titled “Tabs”

The tab and its area live in the URL, so a link opens the same view. A tab you may not see is hidden; a link to it opens the first one you can.

Tab What it shows Needs
Overview Supply chain, Quality and Delivery cards, the freshness of every analysis, facts and the latest audit events. analyses:read
Supply chain One area at a time: Security, Libraries, Licenses, Stack, Public API. analyses:read, Security also audits:read
Quality One area at a time: Coverage, Bundles, Assets, CI, Branches, Reviews. analyses:read
Releases The repository’s release timeline. releases:read
Activity Its audit events, as the audit log shows them to you.
Knowledge Its Guide: the effective prompt and the sections with the layers each comes from. knowledge:read
Settings Every setting as it applies here. See Repository settings. settings:read

Supply chain and Quality areas

Section titled “Supply chain and Quality areas”

Each area is the fleet page’s table narrowed to this repository, with Open in Supply › or Open in Quality linking to the fleet page filtered to it. Stack links to its group’s Stack & conventions tab instead.

Check how fresh the analyses are

Section titled “Check how fresh the analyses are”

The Overview lists the newest record of every analyzer with its state:

State Meaning
Current The record describes the head commit.
Behind Newer commits landed on the default branch since.
Aging Older than usual for its analyzer.
Stale Due for a refresh; Holtkeeper queues one on its own.
Failed The last run failed.
Never The analyzer never ran on this repository.

Run analyzer refreshes one at once; follow the job in Ops › Runs.

Note

Access in the facts says whether Holtkeeper may write to the repository: Read-only with the reason when a freeze applies, or Writable here with the reason when a narrower setting lifts a broader freeze. Read-only repositories get no merge requests from agent tasks.
