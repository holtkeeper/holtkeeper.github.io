GuideHome

Inbox

In the panel: Home › Inbox/inbox

Every decision waiting for you in one list — task proposals, notes, learnings, Guide text, release acceptances, allowlist renewals and budget warnings — with approve, reject and snooze. The Inbox is the second tab of Home and open to every role; it lists only what you may decide.

Two sections, each oldest first: what you decide in your teams, then what your teams can also decide.

What lands here

Section titled “What lands here”
Kind The decision
Task proposals Approve lets the agent change code; nothing runs before. Reject drops the proposal.
Notes Accept or Reject a proposed knowledge note.
Learnings Accept a learned rule into the scope it names (or the suggested one), or Reject it.
Guide Accept or Reject text Holtkeeper generated for a Guide section.
Releases Accept a release that waits for an acceptance, or Reject it: the item hides until new commits arrive and nothing changes on the forge.
Allowlist Renew an allowlist entry by 30 days past its last day or today. It has no Reject.
Budget Acknowledge a weekly AI budget warning, shown to people who may change that budget. It snoozes the warning until the week ends.

Proposing, tagging and deploying a release are not Inbox decisions; they stay on the Releases page.

Find the right items

Section titled “Find the right items”
  • Waiting for you holds what you may decide in your home groups; Your teams can also decide holds the rest. Without home groups everything is in the first section.
  • For me drops the teams section; My teams shows both.
  • The kind segments narrow the list to one kind, each with its count.
  • Sort picks oldest first (the default), newest first or soonest to lapse.
  • The search in the header matches the title, scope, detail, repositories and who proposed it.

The view lives in the URL, so a link opens the same filter and, with an item selected, the same sheet.

Note

The count on the rail’s Home icon and on the Inbox tab is the number of open items, snoozed ones left out. It is the same number the rows add up to.

Decide one item

Section titled “Decide one item”
  1. Click a row, or press J to open the first one. A side sheet opens.
  2. Read the details: what was asked and where it writes, the diff, the rule or the text that would change.
  3. Press A to approve or accept, R to reject, or S to snooze.
  4. Move on with J and K.

What the sheet shows depends on the kind:

Kind In the sheet
Task proposal Diff, Plan, Context (what was asked, by whom and where it writes) and Repositories.
Note The note and an Effective prompt tab: what agents will read once it is accepted.
Learning The rule, its Target or Suggested scope, and a scope picker to accept it somewhere else.
Guide A line diff of the generated text against the accepted text.
Release The release card with the next version and what it contains.
Allowlist A renewal form with the new last day and its reason.
Budget The week’s spend against the budget.

The ⋯ menu snoozes the item or opens it where it lives. The short id in the header copies on click.

Renew an allowlist entry

Section titled “Renew an allowlist entry”

The new last day starts 30 days out. The server refuses a date further away than the allowlist policy allows, or a blank reason when the policy asks for one, and the notice says which rule it broke.

Decide several at once

Section titled “Decide several at once”

Select rows with their checkboxes, then use Approve selected, Reject selected or Snooze 1 day. Items are sent one after another; rows that fail stay selected with their error under the title.

Snooze

Section titled “Snooze”

Snooze an item for a day or a week from the sheet, the ⋯ menu or with S. It moves to the Snoozed tab and comes back on its own. Snoozing is private and decides nothing.

Caution

While Agents may change code is off on Ops › Tasks, task approvals are disabled and a warning says so in the list and in the task sheet.

Note

Decisions are made by people only. No MCP tool approves a task group, accepts a note, learning or Guide text, dismisses a release or renews an allowlist entry.
