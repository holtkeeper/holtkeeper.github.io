GuideHome

Overview

In the panel: Home › Overview/

See in one place what needs you, what changed this week, how your repositories are doing and how much of the AI budget is spent. Home is the first page after sign-in and is open to every role; each card shows only what your permissions let through.

The strip sums the repositories in the current scope; the right end counts analyses that are not current.

Pick the scope

Section titled “Pick the scope”

The switch at the top narrows every card on Home at once.

  • My teams shows the repositories of your home groups. Pick them on Account › My teams.
  • All I can see shows every repository your grants reach.
  • Group: Any ▾ narrows Home to one group; Clear goes back to My teams.

Your choice is remembered in this browser. Items about no repository, such as a budget warning, show in every position; an item about several repositories shows when any of them is in scope.

Tip

If you have not picked any teams, the switch reads My teams 0 and a Choose your teams link appears. Until then Needs attention, What changed and Repositories point you there instead of showing an empty card.

Work through Needs attention

Section titled “Work through Needs attention”

The table lists what Holtkeeper thinks a person should look at, most urgent first:

Kind When it shows
Job failures An analyzer failed in the last 24 hours, grouped by analyzer and error.
Budget stops An AI budget stopped jobs in the last 24 hours.
Vulnerabilities Critical and high findings of a repository’s latest audit, with the change since the last.
Allowlist An allowlist entry you may edit expired or expires within 7 days.
Releases A failed read or deploy, an acceptance nobody gave, or a version blocked by a gate.
Learnings and notes Proposed rules and knowledge entries you may decide.
Stale or missing The newest analysis is older than 14 days, or a repository has never been analysed.

Each row has one action:

  • Retry queues the analyzer again on every repository of the item. It shows only when you may run analyzers on all of them; the arrow beside it opens the item.
  • Review and Renew open the decision in the Inbox.
  • Open goes to the page that explains the item.

Group the rows with By severity, By kind or By repository in the card’s header.

Snooze an item

Section titled “Snooze an item”

Snooze a row for a day when you know about it and will get to it later. Snoozed rows are hidden and left out of the strip’s count. Turn on Snoozed N in the card’s header to see them again, tagged Snoozed after the rest of their group, and unsnooze any of them there.

Note

Snoozing is private: it hides the item for you only and is not a decision. An allowlist item shares its snooze with the Inbox.

Read the other cards

Section titled “Read the other cards”

Fleet health, the week's AI spend and the audit log of the last 7 days.

Card What it shows Needs
Fleet health One tick per repository in scope, worst first, the count per status and how many analyses are stale. repos:read
AI spend this week Spend of the broadest budget you may read against its weekly limit, split by product feature, with a marker where automatic work stops, the projection, the reserve and Resets Monday. ai:read
What changed One sentence per notable event of the last 7 days: versions, deploys, setting changes, accepted rules, new repositories and the like. Full history opens the audit log.
Repositories The ten worst repositories in scope with one cell per health area you may read. repos:read

In Repositories, a health cell opens that area of the repository hub (Security, Libraries, Coverage, Bundles, CI, Releases…) and the path opens its Overview. The footer names the scope and links to the full list when there are more.
